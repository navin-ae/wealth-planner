Important. This is a planning illustration, not investment advice, and no figure here is a forecast or a guarantee. Every projection assumes a constant annual return, steady inflation and uninterrupted contributions, compounded monthly — real markets deliver none of those. Returns are shown before tax, exit load and fund expenses; capital gains on redemption will reduce what you actually receive.

Per section. The safe withdrawal band is guidance, not a rule: its floor withdraws only your inflation-adjusted return so the corpus is preserved, while its ceiling drains the corpus to roughly zero at the end of your SWP period. Target Corpus, Goals and Retirement solve backwards from assumptions you supply — change the return or inflation and the required SIP moves sharply, so treat the inputs as the real decision. The 4% withdrawal rule originates in US market history and may not hold for Indian markets or for retirements longer than 30 years. Monte Carlo draws returns from a normal distribution, which understates the fat tails and sequence-of-returns risk of real markets; its success rate is a comparison tool, not a probability you should bank on.

Figures in Today's value are discounted at your stated inflation rate. Mutual fund investments are subject to market risk; read all scheme-related documents carefully. Please confirm any plan with a SEBI-registered investment adviser before acting on it.
